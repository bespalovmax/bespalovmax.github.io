Drains

Blocked drain solutions

One of the most common plumbing issues that many people face is the blockage of drains. Drains face a daily buildup of grease, hair, soap, detergents, along with other chemicals, making you likely to have an unexpected clog or backup that happens with most residential drains.

GARRY THE PLUMBER specialises in the unclogging of drains of all types.

Armed with extensive experience in plumbing and the commitment for top-notch service and quality workmanship, we can offer extensive drain cleaning and repair services on the following:

  • check_circleToilets
  • check_circleShowers
  • check_circleKitchen sinks
  • check_circleSewers and storm water drains

We can pinpoint your clog and backup problems and easily solve your drain problem. Moreover, we work very tidily and we take every precaution to prevent undue damage to the existing plumbing systems.

Roofing

Roof and guttering

The roof can expose your house to the elements. Therefore, it is important to trust only professionals for your roof and gutter plumbing works.

At GARRY THE PLUMBER, we have the required skills and experience to evaluate your roof needs. We can undertake the installation and replacement of old metal roofs on the house or garage.

We are fully competent to detect and sort out spouting, guttering and downpipe problems and we can carry out any necessary replacement. We also perform roof and leak inspection and we can replace deck and pergola roofs with polycarbonate roofs.

If you have a problem with your guttering and need it cleaned, repaired or replaced please contact us.
