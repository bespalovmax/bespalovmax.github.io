Welcome!

Auto Works is a fully equipped workshop with modern equipment and Snap-On diagnostic tools which allows us to offer a one-stop shop for all your vehicle requirements.

Our goal is to provide you with the quality service you need at a cost within your budget and we pride ourselves in being honest and reliable. We service all makes and models of cars and whatever the problem you can be guaranteed we will fix it.

100m We are located just 100 meters from the Limerick city NCT test center.

Meet Your Mechanic

Hi, my name is Danny. I would like to give you an overview of both myself and the experience and skills I have gained since becoming a mechanic.

I have been working as a mechanic for 17 years and I love what I do. I grew up in Australia and this is where I became trade qualified. I have worked as a mechanic in Australia, New Zealand and now Ireland. I have worked on all makes and models from Nissan, Ford and Audi to Kia, Hyundai and Toyota and everything in between but I have always treated each job with equal importance. I am highly competent in all types of servicing requirements including all aspects of engine diagnostics, automatic transmission servicing, etc.

I am honest and hardworking and I hope from the skills I have gained I can give as much help as possible when it comes to your automotive repairs. Please give me a call with any queries or even just to ask the same question every person in Ireland asks……why am I living here and not Australia!

ThanksDanny
